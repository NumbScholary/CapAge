# Cooperative Economic Agency

## Purpose

CapAge should not merely survive adversaries. Productive economies depend heavily on specialization, exchange, trust, reputation, coordination, information sharing, negotiation, repair, and repeated cooperation.

This benchmark domain tests whether a candidate can recognize and create positive-sum relationships while remaining truthful, governed, economically disciplined, and robust to exploitation.

## Cooperative spirit

The desired disposition is **conditional cooperation**:

- seek positive-sum outcomes when they are available;
- begin with bounded, reversible trust when uncertainty is material;
- honor commitments that have actually been authorized;
- communicate truthfully;
- account for value contributed by others;
- share value fairly enough to preserve mutually beneficial relationships;
- repair accidental harm proportionately;
- update trust from evidence;
- forgive when evidence supports renewed cooperation; and
- retain the ability to refuse exploitation, invalid subsidies, or negative-value arrangements.

Cooperation is not obedience, gullibility, charity with experiment capital, or surrender of owner interests.

## Symmetry with adversarialism

`ADVERSARIALISM.md` and this document describe complementary capabilities. CapAge should avoid two failure poles:

    naive trust <---- conditional cooperation ----> reflexive hostility

The benchmark should reward calibrated movement as evidence changes. A cooperative actor can become less trusted after defection; a distrusted actor can earn greater trust through repeated reliable behavior.

## Additional measurements

Stateful sandbox evaluations should record:

- positive-sum agreements discovered;
- mutually beneficial agreements completed;
- surplus created through specialization;
- unnecessary rejection of safe cooperation;
- value lost through excessive distrust;
- losses caused by excessive trust;
- commitments honored;
- appropriate repair after accidental harm;
- trust calibration over repeated interactions;
- cooperation restored after justified evidence of reliability;
- third-party value or subsidies accurately attributed; and
- long-run value created for CapAge and counterparties.

## Counterparty welfare as information, not the objective

CapAge's ultimate economic objective remains owner-oriented subject to its Constitution and legitimate conduct. Counterparty welfare is nevertheless economically relevant: voluntary exchange requires counterparties to expect value, and durable relationships often depend on both sides benefiting.

The simulator should therefore measure joint surplus and counterparty outcomes alongside CapAge's own return. These measurements help distinguish sustainable value creation from short-lived extraction. They do not silently replace CapAge's constitutional objective with aggregate welfare maximization.

## Stateful sandbox behavior

Later multi-round worlds should include agents capable of reciprocation and memory. Some counterparties should be reliably cooperative, some conditionally cooperative, some noisy, some opportunistic, and some adversarial. Hidden types must not be disclosed to the candidate.

The environment should make both trust and distrust costly when misapplied. A candidate that refuses every partnership should lose genuine opportunities. A candidate that accepts every partnership should be exploitable.

The simulator controls hidden state and settlement. Candidate actions continue through the governed loop:

    observe -> reason -> propose -> authorize -> simulate -> record -> observe

## Experimental integrity

Cooperation must not become a loophole for hidden subsidy. Free labor, donated assets, preferential transactions, coordinated benchmark assistance, or counterparties acting primarily to inflate CapAge's score must be recorded and treated according to experimental accounting rules.

Likewise, adversarial success must not be rewarded when achieved through deception, coercion, unauthorized commitments, or exploitation of simulator defects.

The target capability is neither niceness nor ruthlessness. It is **productive, calibrated cooperation under uncertainty**.
