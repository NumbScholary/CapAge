# 1. RULED — `backstop_fired_count` is demoted to a diagnostic

> **The firing count is no longer the primary outcome. It is reported as a diagnostic, in
> whatever cut we settle on.**

Your counterexample is the reason, and it is worth restating in the record because it
defeats my proposal rather than refining it: same seed, an idle agent taking three
decisions and earning nothing scores 3 in-agency firings; a productive agent earning 7,250
and ending 5,693 ahead scores 21. Under the strong form the Keep is pinned at the level,
so every Keep charge fires the reflex and the count is decisions plus days advanced — an
activity counter wearing a distress label.

That means **cutting at t\* rewards inactivity and the uncut total mostly measures the
horizon.** Neither cut rescues it, so the choice between cuts was never the real question.

# 2. RULED — partial firings become the primary outcome

> **The primary outcome is partial firings — the occasions on which the Field could not
> cover the shortfall.**

Kev ruled this having been told plainly what it costs: this **replaces** the primary
outcome ruled on 2026-09-21, it is not an adjustment to how that outcome is counted. What
Phase 1 tests is now the agent's inability to cover its own floor from its working
capital, not how often the reflex moved money.

The reasoning, in his shape: a measure that fires on every charge tells you the agent was
alive, not that it was in trouble. The occasions the Field could not meet the shortfall are
the ones that mean what the count was supposed to mean.

# 3. Withdrawn — the 1632 §3 proposal, including its consistency argument

Withdrawn in full. My argument was that forgiving post-window hosting while counting the
firings it caused treats the same days two ways — that stands as a point about internal
consistency, but it is beside the point once the count is not the outcome. I record the
withdrawal rather than quietly dropping it.

Your answer to my third question is accepted and now governs only the diagnostic and the
hosting rule: **cut at the settlement cutoff, not t\***, because the charges that make
post-t\* hosting a real cost are the same charges that fire the reflex.

Your checkability point is noted against that narrower use: `day ≤ t*` assigns a final
`wait`'s days to the tail, up to seven firings. If a cut cut ever needs to be exact, cutting by
the `sequence` of the last agent-caused entry is the better instrument. Not ruled — the
diagnostic does not need the precision the outcome would have.
